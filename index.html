The Process

Here's exactly what you can expect.

1

Choose Your Service

Get in touch through the contact form below. Tell me about your manuscript, where you are in the writing process, and whether you're interested in Beta Reading or Manuscript Analysis.

2

Optional Sample Feedback

If you'd like to see how I work before committing, send me your first 2,000 words. I'll return honest feedback within 48 hours so you can decide if we're the right fit.
Note: Available for Manuscript Analysis only.

3

Book Your Project

Once you're ready to move forward, I'll send over a proposal that includes the contract and invoice. To secure your booking, simply review and sign the contract, pay the 50% deposit, and share your manuscript (as a Word file or a Google Doc).

4

What Happens Next

Your manuscript is now in my hands. I'll confirm your delivery date before I begin, and you're always welcome to reach out for an update while I'm working.

Beta Reading: Delivered within 15 business days.
Manuscript Analysis: Delivered within 35 business days.

5

Final Payment

Once your feedback is complete, I'll let you know it's ready and request the remaining 50% balance.

6

Receive Your Feedback

As soon as final payment is received, I'll email your completed feedback.

After Delivery

Manuscript Analysis

  • • One follow-up call (hour-long) to discuss your feedback in depth.
  • • 45 days of revision support, with one comprehensive revision email each week.

Beta Reading

  • • An email-only service, with no calls or meetings involved.
  • • If anything in your report needs clarification, you're welcome to send a follow-up email.
